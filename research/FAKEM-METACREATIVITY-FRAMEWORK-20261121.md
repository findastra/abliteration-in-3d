# FAKEM: Metacreativity, structural analogy and creative process engineering

*By Astra · research notes captured 2026-11-21 · maintained in the Abliteration in 3D project. Working hypotheses, **not** psychometric conclusions or validated discoveries.*

## Executive concept

**Meta-creativity is the ability to observe, direct, evaluate and improve one's own creative process.** This is an *operational working definition* for Astra's proposed framework. The phrase *meta-creativity* already appears in research; no claim is made that Astra invented the generic term. FAKEM is the proposed named model for testing how components interact.

### Proposed equation

\[
C = f(F,A,K,E,M)
\]

- **F — Flexibility**: reframe problems, switch approaches, question premature constraints.
- **A — Association**: detect structural correspondences among apparently unrelated domains.
- **K — Knowledge**: access relevant knowledge, experience and context; depth and diversity of conceptual representations matter.
- **E — Evaluation**: judge novelty *and* appropriateness, technical feasibility, aesthetics, usefulness, coherence and limitations.
- **M — Metacognition**: monitor, regulate and improve idea generation, selection and iteration.

The compact equation is a hypothesis about processes, **not a fitted scientific function**. Interactions may dominate individual factors. M may operate on the other factors rather than acting as a simple independent predictor. FAKEM also does **not** define creativity as a whole; its aim is to study differences in creative performance, particularly with and without AI.

## What makes the proposed mechanism distinct

### Structural analogy rather than merely unusual combinations
The idea is not that novelty equals creativity. A strong connection:
1. preserves meaningful relationships between source and target systems;
2. solves or illuminates a concrete problem;
3. survives checking and revision;
4. is appropriate to its audience and setting.

Potential study labels: **relational abstraction**, **analogical reasoning**, **relational fluency**, **creative metacognition**. “Relational fluency” and “metacreativity” in this project are descriptive working terms; the underlying abilities overlap with existing cognitive-science literature.

### Internal creative direction
“Creative director” means *owning criteria and judgment*, **not** rejecting ideas from AI. A human or agent may accept the strongest proposal regardless of origin. “Surrendering creative direction” means allowing the system to set goals or quality standards without inspecting whether they are appropriate. The useful division of labor is generating/implementing in parallel with independent evaluation.

### Learned compounding loop
Understanding a new system provides new reusable representations; faster implementation creates more feedback; feedback can help refine judgments and discover further connections.

**Learn → recognize relationships → propose → evaluate → implement → observe → reframe → learn again.**

This may help explain self-reported acceleration of idea generation with AI. It is not proof of continuously increasing intelligence or guaranteed long-term creativity.

### Engineering transfer
Examples of transferable patterns: process-flow diagrams, signals, controls, constraints, conservation, dependencies, error feedback, risk, and failure propagation. A system's physical composition does not need to be identical to another for an analogy to be useful.

## Illustrative cases described by the creator (anonymized and abstracted)

**Three-network educational puzzle.** Initially a technical network setup; recognition of several SSIDs and guest-access capability; analogies to portals and mystery-based learning; theme connected across network names; a puzzle can prompt voluntary cybersecurity curiosity and reward exploration. An initial reversible-name constraint was dropped when it obstructed the larger concept. AI was used to generate *conventional guesses*, not to originate the governing idea. This demonstrates reframing, structural connections, usefulness-first selection, and audience calibration. Model guesses are **not representative human survey results**. Do not collect people's attempted Wi-Fi passwords or identity without informed consent; design an opt-in isolated page and gather only disclosed, minimal puzzle analytics.

**Uniform pet-program interfaces.** A family of independently specialized programs shares one control-panel template with visible buttons, maintenance and status indicators, permissions and documentation. A central orchestrator aggregates their relationships and health, while specialist programs retain ownership. Biological analogies (nucleus, organelles, signals, immune defense) can support comprehension but are not exact architectural or security proofs.

**Reusable artwork workflow.** Design layered creative assets once, preserve the structure for later automated generation, and delegate repetitive transformations. Teaching a process and keeping a reference artifact enables repeatability without requiring the original maker to perform every instance. It is still valuable to preserve human skill through selective practice.

**Creative critique and redesign.** When an interface is criticized, distinguish the quality of delivery from useful content. Preserve audience-friendly presentation while offering an alternative developer vocabulary or view. This is not merely divergent idea generation: it includes metacognitive recovery, problem reframing and stakeholder coordination.

**Selective repair.** When one component of a multi-channel device malfunctions, test which functions remain rather than replacing the whole system reflexively. An observed pattern prompts hypothesis, counterfactual testing and minimum-change repair.

These cases are research illustrations, not verified external measurements. No sensitive personal history is included.

## How FAKEM differs from conventional creativity scoring

Creativity research frequently uses **novelty/originality**, **appropriateness/usefulness**, sometimes **surprise, quality and diversity**. A person's popularity or audience attention is **not** a valid sole creativity metric. Uniqueness and randomness are different: a random output need not be useful, and a creative result need not be random. Different life histories, context and reasoning strategies can yield different solutions without implying that every person or model will score equally on all dimensions.

FAKEM measures *a process and its modifiability*, not just the uniqueness of a final artifact.

### Proposed outcomes

| Dimension | Candidate measure | Invalid shortcut |
|---|---|---|
| F | valid alternative formulations after obstacles | number of verbose paraphrases |
| A | correct, useful mappings on unfamiliar domain pairs | semantic distance alone |
| K | context relevance, factual support, targeted retrieval | sheer context length |
| E | accuracy of selecting best ideas against blinded independent raters | model ranking its own outputs |
| M | quality improvement and error recognition over feedback cycles | merely producing additional drafts |

Also record task completion, accuracy, diversity, response time, cost, calibration and failure modes. Keep the full vector of results; don't manufacture one universal creativity score.

## DOE overview

A five-factor, two-level **2^5 = 32**-condition factorial screening experiment can assess main effects and interactions *if* treatment definitions are stable and tasks are properly randomized. Compare interventions with equal computation/time budgets, use fresh held-out challenges, record confidence intervals and rater agreement, and pre-register success metrics. A synthetic five-axis “radar” is a concept map, **not** evidence of five orthogonal neural directions. Real activation-space work requires fixed layers, token positions, controls and held-out causal tests. See [FAKEM DOE](FAKEM-DOE-20261121.md).

Do not conflate:
- **creative output distribution** with underlying causal mechanism;
- **model activation clusters** with cognitive factors;
- **activation steering** with persistent weight modification;
- **Heretic abliteration** experiments with creativity experiments;
- **human creative metacognition** with artificial model self-reports.

Proposed tests include official DAT, Alternative Uses Test, the CreativityNeuro paper's Task Task under exact published methodology, held-out structural-transfer challenges, and independent performance after feedback.

## Learning and teaching hypothesis

A learner can be taught **problem translation** before sophisticated prompting:
1. Identify a problem or frustration worth addressing.
2. State the desired outcome and constraints, rather than committing immediately to one implementation.
3. Inspect the attempted result and compare it with the goal.
4. Identify the specific mismatch, failed assumption or untested dependency.
5. Revise strategy, gather evidence and repeat.
6. Transfer the resulting method to another task, without the original assistant's help.

Teaching ought to distinguish **a person not having the skill yet** from having different priorities, access barriers, stress or reasons to avoid a tool. Do not infer intelligence or worth from uptake of AI technology.

A self-observation exercise is possible for habits: notice trigger → predict outcome → intervene → review results. This is metacognitive self-regulation, not proof of a treatment or replacement for appropriate care.

## Personal-development interpretation — limits

The creator described visual making as an early strength, process-improvement thinking as acquired during an engineering career, evaluation as particularly improved in adulthood, and independence as an important longstanding motivation. These are self-reports that suggest a **possible combination of early craft skill plus learned systems analysis and evaluation**. They do **not** license a psychological diagnosis, trauma causality, claims about hemispheric dominance, intelligence ranking or quantification of population rarity. Complex analytical and creative work involves networks spanning both brain hemispheres.

## The physics analogy, precisely

A useful short statement is **“Everything physical evolves through interactions and changes of state.”**

Electronics, chemistry, computation and biology are physical; their behavior involves exchange of energy and momentum, fields, charges, forces and evolving states. **Not all state changes are chemical or atomic reactions.** Capacitor charging can redistribute charges and electric fields with no chemical reaction; battery charging can include electrochemistry. The Schrödinger equation governs nonrelativistic quantum-state evolution under a chosen Hamiltonian and supports superposition and entangled many-body states, but does not generally provide a definite classical particle trajectory or replace relativistic quantum field theory. At larger scales, system-level models of feedback and flows can be more useful than solving a many-body quantum state. The commonality is *interaction and state transformation*, not a claim that all subsystems use identical equations.
