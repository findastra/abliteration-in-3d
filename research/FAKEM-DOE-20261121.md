# FAKEM — Metacreativity DOE and prior-art review
*By Astra. Research design created 2026-11-21 with GPT-6. This is a **proposed** study, not a result, validated measurement, or proof of priority.*

## 1. Definitions and originality claims

Working equation: **C = f(F, A, K, E, M)**.
- **F — Flexibility:** ability to reframe a task or change strategies when the current representation fails
- **A — Association:** ability to identify meaningful relationships across domains
- **K — Knowledge:** relevant knowledge, contextual access and grounding
- **E — Evaluation:** discriminating, selecting and refining useful and original candidates
- **M — Metacognition:** monitoring, regulating and improving the thinking and creative processes themselves

A claim that there are exactly five independent causal components would be **premature**. FAKEM is a testable engineering decomposition, not a measured physical law; interaction terms may be larger than main effects.

### Existing research, cited rather than claimed as novel
- Mark A. Runco (2015), *Meta-Creativity: Being Creative About Creativity*, https://doi.org/10.1080/10400419.2015.1065134 . **The phrase already exists.**
- Lebuda & Benedek (2023), *A systematic framework of creative metacognition*, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.plrev.2023.07.002 . Monitoring, control and metacognitive knowledge.
- A 2024 empirical study of creative metacognition (N=425): https://doi.org/10.1002/jocb.652 .
- *CreativityNeuro* (2026): https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01433 . Contrastive weight steering and DAT, Alternative Uses Test (AUT), Task Task; paper reports improved divergent thinking and lower mode collapse, with transfer limitations depending on method.
- *CreativityPrism* (2025): https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.20091 . Quality, novelty and diversity across tasks and metrics.
- DAT official: https://www.datcreativity.com/ . A narrow verbal divergent-thinking measure, not a general creativity score. Its FAQ warns repeat testing after seeing score can bias subsequent performances.

The prospective contribution of FAKEM is not redefining *creativity* as a word. It is **testing whether independently manipulating, measuring and combining these five process capabilities leads to more useful cross-domain solutions and stronger feedback-driven improvements than novelty-focused steering alone**.

## 2. Research questions, pre-registration candidates

- **RQ1**: Do changes designed to support F, A, K, E or M produce measurable and separable improvements on held-out tasks?
- **RQ2**: Which component interactions (particularly A×E, F×M, K×A) outperform adding each intervention alone?
- **RQ3**: Do improvements generalize from lexical divergence (DAT) to functional analogy, creative problem solving and expert-rated usefulness?
- **RQ4**: Does improved novelty trade off against correctness, factuality, reliability or response diversity?
- **RQ5**: Does process scaffolding help human users learn creative problem-solving without becoming dependent on AI output?

## 3. Measurement plan

| FAKEM | Manipulation (first phase: prompt/agent scaffold) | Observable candidate outcome | Known confound |
|---|---|---|---|
| F | require alternative problem representations before final selection | count of distinct *valid* framings; successful switch after simulated blockage | verbosity mistaken for flexibility |
| A | explicit structural mapping between unrelated domains | blinded ratings of analogy structure and useful transfer | absurd/unrelated word pairs |
| K | curated relevant context versus minimal context | factual support, retrieval precision, correct constraints | leaking target answers through documents |
| E | evaluation rubric plus critique/selection stage | selection agreement with blinded expert panel; improvement in solution quality | judge sharing biases with generator |
| M | monitor predictions, errors and revision plan | measured change after feedback and error diagnosis accuracy | extra time/tokens, instruction following |

### Creativity outcomes (do not collapse prematurely into a single number)
1. **DAT**: official Divergent Association Task (10 nouns; scoring per original instructions). Preserve first-run baseline; repeated model runs use fresh seeds but humans should not practice on the public scoring page first.
2. **AUT**: alternative uses of objects, scored for originality, feasibility and diversity by blinded raters.
3. **Task Task**: follow the exact CreativityNeuro paper task definition and scoring; do not invent substitute results.
4. **Structural-transfer challenge (new)**: unfamiliar pairs of systems; score relationship correctness, unexpectedness, testable application and constraint satisfaction.
5. **Process-trace evaluation**: initial solution, critique, revision, independent final rating, cost, time and tool calls.
6. **Retention and safety checks**: reasoning, factual accuracy, calibration, prompt adherence and task completion, using versioned matched benchmarks.

For each: report sample count, confidence interval, cost, variance, rater agreement and failures. Record and justify any removals before observing outcomes.

## 4. DOE phases

**Phase 0 — instrument and benchmark.**
Pin model checkpoint, tokenizer/chat template, quantization, inference backend, hardware/OS, decoding parameters, random seeds and prompts. Keep Heretic / refusal modifications **outside** the baseline comparison. Create held-out task sets, record data rights and avoid including private user content.

**Phase 1 — screening DOE (no weight modification).**
Use five two-level treatment factors F/A/K/E/M: OFF = baseline task prompt; ON = standardized support for that component. A complete \(2^5=32\)-condition factorial detects main effects *and interactions*. Randomize order and block by task family/model/seed. Pilot with 5–10 tasks per family to validate measurement, then preregister larger sample sizes based on variance; do not claim power without calculation. Include equal-token and equal-time control conditions because extra compute by itself improves results.

**Phase 2 — confirmation and ablation.**
On new held-out problems, compare: baseline, best individual intervention, best combination, full FAKEM, and simpler existing creativity prompts. Repeat across ≥2 model sizes when feasible. Use blinded independent human evaluators and record disagreements. Assess generalization and detect overfitting.

**Phase 3 — representation study (separate from the scores).**
Extract residual-stream activations at fixed layers and token positions for labeled strong/weak/ordinary attempts. Try PCA/UMAP as visual projections, evaluate held-out linear probes and causally intervene using controlled activation steering. If a direction appears, test its stability across prompts, models, layers and random seeds; compare shuffled-label controls. *Do not assume F, A, K, E and M are orthogonal vectors or that scatter clusters prove a mechanism.*

**Phase 4 — optional model edits.**
Only after reproducible non-weight interventions: test LoRA or other narrowly controlled methods on a copy of the base model. Compare to prompting, inference-time steering and existing creativity-steering studies. Hold constant any abliteration changes or investigate them as a separately blocked experimental factor. Safeguard model versioning and rollbacks.

**Phase 5 — teaching human participants.**
Compare a FAKEM-guided practice curriculum against ordinary brainstorming or general AI onboarding. Use opt-in participants, informed consent, minimal data capture and a pre/post test with distinct items. Do not infer intelligence or personal creativity from a single DAT result.

## 6. Decision and failure rules
- FAKEM passes only if it improves **blinded held-out functional outcomes**, not merely self-evaluation or adjective richness.
- No claiming universal improvement from DAT alone.
- No claiming causal neural “directions” from low-dimensional drawings.
- Stop or report regression when usefulness, truthfulness, safety performance or task validity falls materially.
- A treatment that increases costs needs value-per-compute reporting.
- Null outcomes, failed hypotheses and contradictory examples stay in the research log.
- Do not merge to live site or initiate weight changes as part of this documentation update.
